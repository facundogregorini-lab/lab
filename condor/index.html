03 · Process

From brief to final cut

  1. 01

    Brief

    Tell us the place, the story and the deadline. We reply with a plan and a quote.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Airspace, permissions, weather, tides and the angle of the sun, all checked before anyone drives out.

  3. 03

    Fly

    We capture at the best light, with several passes and angles, plus stills while we're up there.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Edited and colour-graded footage, ready for the web, the big screen and your phone.

04 · Story

From the Andes to the Coast Mountains.

The Andean condor is one of the largest flying birds on the planet, with wings that reach 3.3 metres. It barely flaps them: it reads the wind, rides the thermals and sees the whole mountain range at once.

Condor was born in Argentina and took off in Vancouver. The Sun of May and the maple leaf share the same emblem because this project is both: southern eyes looking at the Pacific Northwest from above.
